# Computech — specialist motorsport electronics benchmarks

**Recorded:** 18 November 2026 (Australia/Melbourne)  
**Reason:** Owner spotted Computech's Facebook page and asked to retain the specific products discussed as R&D references.  
**Status:** Competitor/product-range research only. No new SkidLabz product selected, no existing project reopened, no development or sourcing authorised.

## Company position

[Computech](https://computech.com/) is already listed in R-D as both a developer/own-product supplier and a specialist retailer ([manufacturers](../competitors/MANUFACTURERS.md#computech), [resellers](../competitors/RESELLERS.md#computech)). Its [about page](https://computech.com/about/) describes its origins in drag-racing weather analysis and its own weather stations, data-acquisition systems and EGT monitors, alongside products from other brands. It is a **specialist motorsport-electronics product-range and positioning reference**, not an established small-batch OEM supplier.

### Own products versus items sold by Computech

- Computech markets its **EGT Plus, DataMaxx and RaceAir Pro** as its branded systems. This does not establish where every physical component is manufactured.
- Some other catalogue items are **resold third-party designs**: for example, the [US$359.99 Switch Panel](https://computech.com/product/switch-panel/) explicitly identifies **Digital Delay** as the designer. Do not count all Computech switch panels or delay boxes as Computech's own R&D.
- These products illustrate a premium, focused motorsport-electronics range with accessories, sensing and support. They do **not** establish viable low-volume SkidLabz manufacturing economics or a gap in Australian demand.
