## Idea

Affordable stand-alone race dash with a daylight-readable screen and a simple core instrument set.

## Why it is at this status

No suitably priced display has been established with adequate daylight readability, integrated controller/carrier and accessible front mounting.

## Next useful step

Resolve a purchasable display against the existing requirements before proceeding with PCB or case work.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

A suitable display with verified size, mounting, daylight performance, price and small-quantity supply.

## Names and search terms

Full race dash, TFT dash, PDM CAN dash/status display

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Parked, not abandoned.
- Do not substitute a bare ribbon panel or a dim framed HMI and call the screen problem solved.
- Keep the PDM CAN status-display option related to this entry; it is not a second approved dash project.
