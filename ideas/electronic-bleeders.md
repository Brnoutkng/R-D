## Idea

Rechargeable electronic pressure-control units using the centre-axle installation and two tyre hoses.

## Why it is at this status

Practical retention, economical valve supply, pressure-control validation and overall assembly cost remain open.

## Next useful step

Use the retained axle/retention study and finish mechanical/valve feasibility before detailed electronics.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

Credible retention proof, affordable repeatable valve supply and a viable complete-unit cost.

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Rim-mounted TPMS-style bleeder was set aside for rotating mass/force, packaging and test practicality.
- Mechanical tank-and-bleeder route is not the chosen product.
- Latching, servo/motor and proportional alternatives were deferred, not proven production solutions.
- Pump-plus-bleeder is future scope only; no separate product was started.
