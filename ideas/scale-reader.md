## Idea

A reader/interface for a known compatible set of existing corner-weight pads.

## Why it is at this status

Earlier owner rejected retrofit demand because local racers generally did not own suitable scales. Later low-cost reader research did not establish a new market or owner approval.

## Next useful step

Retain the existing decision; no new development action is implied by this register.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

A real installed-base/customer need, known compatible pads and an independently developable implementation.
