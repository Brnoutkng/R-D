## Idea

A wider-pressure-range gauge sharing elements of the precision gauge platform.

## Why it is at this status

It was a future study suggestion; wider range can worsen absolute low-pressure error and does not solve the initial product.

## Next useful step

Retain the existing decision; no new development action is implied by this register.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

The initial gauge is proved and a real wider-range market is selected.
