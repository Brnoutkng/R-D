## Idea

A stand providing gentle heat to keep a racing helmet warm between sessions, aimed at cold-visor fogging during the first laps in cooler Australian speedway conditions.

## Why it is at this status

Steven reports recurring visor fogging and believes existing helmet warmers are expensive or difficult to obtain. Availability, broader demand and effectiveness remain to be independently checked; this is not a development commitment.

## Next useful step

Keep on the idea list. Any later investigation should check alternatives, demand, safe helmet temperatures and effective first-lap benefit. USB power is an open feasibility question, not a requirement or established solution.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

Evidence of a worthwhile customer problem, safe and effective use, and viable complete-product economics; explicit owner decision before development.

## Names and search terms

Helmet warmer, Visor fogging prevention

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- User-proposed research candidate, not an assistant-only suggestion or approved build.
- Do not claim prewarming prevents fogging throughout a race without testing.
- Safe operating temperature, helmet material compatibility and fault protection are unresolved; do not invent a universal temperature limit.
- Mainstream commercial demand takes priority over ease of electronics development.
- Latest owner clarification: list entry only, no large investigation requested; gentle between-session warming on a stand.
