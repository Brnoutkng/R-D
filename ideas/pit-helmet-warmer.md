## Idea

A small stand on a toolbox or trailer bench that keeps a racing helmet gently warm between heats. Steven wants roughly body-temperature warmth as a concept, not an approved temperature limit. No fan or drying function is needed; the goal is to reduce breath fog on a cold visor at the start, not to heat the helmet during use.

## Why it is at this status

Steven sees this only a few times per year, so track testing would be hard to schedule. Commercial helmet warming exists, but the closest verified products use other formats. This remains a maybe, not a development commitment.

No exact compact, low-power, fanless motorsport stand was verified. This is not proof of a market gap or that either benchmark prevents visor fog after removal.

## Next useful step

Park for Steven to review later. If reopened, use a stationary comparison on a spare visor off the helmet: cold versus gently warmed, with the same delay between removal and the breath test. A noticeable, repeatable improvement and demand from other drivers should come before enclosure or electronics work. Verify helmet-maker limits before any whole-helmet heating; [FIA helmet guidance](https://www.fia.com/sites/default/files/guidelines_helmets-to_publish_bd.pdf) warns that high heat can affect the impact foam. USB power is an open question.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

Evidence of a worthwhile customer problem, safe and effective use, and viable complete-product economics; explicit owner decision before development.

## Names and search terms

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- User-proposed research candidate, not an assistant-only suggestion or approved build.
- Do not claim prewarming prevents fogging throughout a race without testing.
- Safe operating temperature, helmet material compatibility and fault protection are unresolved; do not invent a universal temperature limit.
- Mainstream commercial demand takes priority over ease of electronics development.
- Latest owner clarification: list entry only, no large investigation requested; gentle between-session warming on a stand.
