## Idea

A carbon-fibre throttle-linkage bellcrank for sprintcars, based on an existing part the owner has used and tested over several years.

## Why it is at this status

Manufacturing was the earlier blocker. New manufacturing contacts may make the existing design worth revisiting, but production supply, cost and part consistency are not established.

## Next useful step

If revisited, start from the existing part and drawings if available. Establish whether a supplier can reproduce it in an affordable small batch before investing in redesign.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

A credible small-batch manufacturing route that can reproduce the existing part consistently, with production samples checked before sale.

## Names and search terms

Carbon throttle bellcrank, Sprintcar throttle bell crank

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Owner experience applies to the existing part, not to a new supplier's production parts.
- The throttle linkage is safety-critical; verify production fit, durability and free return to idle before release.
- No supplier contact, quote request or purchase approved by this entry.
