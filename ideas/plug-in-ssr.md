## Idea

A solid-state replacement fitting an existing automotive relay position.

## Why it is at this status

Load compatibility, heat, inrush, failure behaviour, packaging and complete economics remain unproven.

## Next useful step

Define the exact replacement relay/load use case and evaluate the physical and commercial limits.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

A defined compatible load/socket scope and costed proof.

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Do not silently expand it into a complete PDM or universal motor controller.
