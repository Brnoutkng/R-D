## Idea

A solid-state replacement fitting an existing automotive relay position.

## Why it is at this status

Owner explicitly deprioritized it for now because of Chinese competition and perceived commercial viability. It remains a possible later add-on, not a current development priority or permanently rejected product.

## Next useful step

Keep as a possible later add-on. Do not start development now; revisit only if the owner sees a credible differentiated commercial case.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

Owner revisits it after evidence of a worthwhile differentiated use case and viable economics despite existing competition; exact load/socket, heat and inrush constraints would still need assessment.

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Do not silently expand it into a complete PDM or universal motor controller.
- Current priority is mainstream commercially viable tech products: dashes, gauges, tools and pit equipment, rather than this relay add-on.
