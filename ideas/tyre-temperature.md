## Idea

Rapid probe-temperature recording and memory for solo tyre checks, potentially for circuit/kart users.

## Why it is at this status

Owner rejected the usefulness of tyre pyrometry for his initial Speedway/dirt-racing context: tyres cool rapidly after exiting, wet track/water can affect the measurement, and he reports logging is prohibited in his classes. Live external sensing would add complexity. A newer broader quick-capture proposal remains unapproved research, evidenced in a secondary recap.

## Next useful step

Retain the existing decision; no new development action is implied by this register.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

A distinct circuit/kart customer need and specific capture advantage, with actual owner adoption.

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Do not describe the newer suggestion as an active development project.
- Infrared surface temperature and probe tyre readings are different use cases.
- Logging restrictions are owner-reported for his classes, not a verified universal Speedway rule. Do not generalize to all classes or disciplines.
- The latest explanation reinforces the existing Speedway rejection; it does not reopen the pyrometer or authorize live sensing.
