## Idea

Carbon-fibre steering box mounts for sprintcars. The owner made working prototypes on his CNC router.

## Why it is at this status

The home-router process took too much owner time for repeat production. New manufacturing contacts may provide an outsourced small-batch route.

## Next useful step

If revisited, start with the existing parts and files. Establish whether a supplier can reproduce them without bringing the labour back in-house.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

A credible small-batch manufacturing route with suitable cost, repeatability, and a qualified validation plan for the finished steering mounts.

## Names and search terms

Carbon sprint steering box mounts, Carbon steering box brackets

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Prototype success is not qualification of outsourced parts.
- Steering mounts are safety-critical. Verify applicable class rules and qualify the finished parts before release.
- No outreach, quotes, or purchases authorised by the entry.
