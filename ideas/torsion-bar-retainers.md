## Idea

Sprintcar torsion bar retainers prototyped using printed PA12-CF and machined carbon-fibre plate. The proposed commercial variant uses an aluminium body with carbon end plates.

## Why it is at this status

The owner still uses the prototypes but was not confident in the threaded printed section and did not pursue it for production. The aluminium-body variant has not been qualified.

## Next useful step

If revisited, assess the aluminium-body variant against the existing prototype and confirm what material and configuration the intended class accepts before commissioning a sample.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

A manufacturable, affordable variant with confirmed class eligibility and a qualified validation plan for the finished retention assembly.

## Names and search terms

Carbon torsion bar retainers, Sprintcar torsion stop retainers

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- This is a retention device, not the torsion bar tester.
- The aluminium-body variant is a proposal, not a tested replacement. Do not infer that putting the threads in metal qualifies the complete assembly.
- SCCA rule 8.9(iii) addresses front torsion stop/bar retention and leaves suitability to the scrutineer; the rule does not name this design or establish a carbon-material exception.
- No outreach, quotes or purchases authorised by the entry.
