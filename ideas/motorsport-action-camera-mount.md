## Idea

A secure action-camera mounting kit for race cars, with a low-profile mechanical mount and independent secondary retention as a candidate design.

## Why it is at this status

The owner has struggled to find mounts he trusts and cites JOES as a credible benchmark. Other dedicated mounts exist; any advantage must be demonstrated for the complete camera-to-car assembly.

## Next useful step

If revisited, pick one camera and installation position; compare JOES with a low-profile mount that can retain the camera even if the primary mount separates.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

An identified fitment or retention advantage, realistic small-batch cost, and a credible test plan covering primary and secondary retention.

## Names and search terms

GoPro race car mount, Roll-cage camera mount

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Candidate only: no design, supplier, approval or batch committed.
- Secondary retention should connect the camera or suitably retained housing to a separate permitted vehicle attachment, rather than only to the primary clamp.
- Confirm location and fastening against the applicable class rules and verify head clearance, exit clearance, vibration and retention before sale. Do not claim blanket race approval.
