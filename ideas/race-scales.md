## Idea

An affordable complete four-pad corner-weight system for grassroots racers.

## Why it is at this status

Quoted supply and pad manufacturing were too costly; local complete products undercut direct-China offers. Large pads, calibration and freight defeated the intended economics.

## Next useful step

Retain the existing decision; no new development action is implied by this register.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

A materially different, verified complete-set or pad supply cost; another electronics-only cost estimate is insufficient.

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Old READY_TO_ORDER labels are stale and do not authorize an order.
- A later limited-pad-reader suggestion did not reactivate the complete-scale product.
