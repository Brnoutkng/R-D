## Idea

A standalone switch that changes an electrical output at a user-set engine RPM.

## Why it is at this status

The first application, buyer demand and advantage over existing switches are not established.

## Next useful step

If revisited, identify the first use case and whether it requires one threshold or separate on/off RPM settings.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

An identified use case and a credible price, ease-of-use or fit advantage over existing options.

## Names and search terms
