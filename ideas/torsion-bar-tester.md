Measure actual torsion-bar rate, identify unmarked bars and track rate changes over time.

## Why it is at this status

No economical trusted reference bar/dyno or verification rig was available; a full rig would consume disproportionate time and cost.

## Next useful step

## Decisions and boundaries to retain
