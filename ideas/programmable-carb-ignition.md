## Idea

A standalone MSD-style ignition box for carburetted engines, with a user-programmable timing curve. An onboard manifold absolute pressure (MAP) sensor is a possible option for load-based timing.

## Why it is at this status

Direct and adjacent products already offer programmable timing and integrated MAP. No first application, competitive advantage or development approval has been established.

## Next useful step

If revisited, identify one target engine/trigger setup and whether the product must replace the ignition box or only control timing with existing ignition hardware.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

A specific use case with an advantage in fit, setup or total installed cost over existing products and a credible validation path.

## Names and search terms

Programmable ignition box, Ignition-only controller

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- No architecture, MAP range, target price or release decision selected.
- Do not treat a timing-only add-on as interchangeable with a replacement capacitive-discharge ignition.
- No development or purchasing implied by the register entry.
- See RD-125 for the separate distributorless multi-coil retrofit concept.
