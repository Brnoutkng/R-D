## Idea

A standalone GPS-based timing instrument.

## Why it is at this status

Owner does not consider a standalone lap timer a mainstream shortlist opportunity, citing MyLaps and Race Monitor on phones as adequate competition for the use he has in mind.

## Next useful step

Do not pursue for the current shortlist. Preserve the owner's market objection.

## What would justify reopening or advancing it

Explicit owner reconsideration based on a specific unmet need and credible advantage over existing alternatives.

## Decisions and boundaries to retain

- Preserved so the suggestion is not presented as new next time.
- Not a current product commitment.
- This is a commercial judgment for the proposed use case, not proof that phone timing/results services and standalone GPS timers have identical functions or that no racers buy dedicated timers.
