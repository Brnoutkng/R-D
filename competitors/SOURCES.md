## How evidence is used

**Historical evidence** establishes that a name appeared in our work. H references are recovered conversation or saved-file summaries; they are not original transcript exports and have no invented chat permalinks. R references are pinned repository documents. Where the original mention was an assistant suggestion, the record remains a historical research reference rather than an owner-approved competitor or supplier.

**Role evidence** links official business pages, product documentation or, where explicitly qualified, a historical brand-authored document/identified partner source. The review verifies identity/role to the stated extent. It is not a current-stock, product-performance, factory audit or price check.

## Exclusions

### Component and fabrication suppliers

Semiconductor, connector, display, valve-only, PCB, enclosure and machining vendors are outside these competitor lists unless a relevant finished-product brand was actually compared. Examples from the recovered material include Bosch sensors, NiceRF modules, Switchcraft, C&K, Waveshare, Nextion, Amflo/Interstate valve leads and generic fabrication vendors. Inclusion in a BOM is not a competitor finding.

### Protocols and open-source reference projects

RMonitor is not a company. Speeduino, rusEFI, SLC-Free/TinyWB, OWBC, oelprinz BL49, mck1117, hbarylskyi LSU4.9 ESP32 and Tuner-DAQ-Box are engineering/project references, not established manufacturer businesses in this review.

### Marketplaces

AliExpress, Alibaba and generic marketplace listings were sourcing channels, not identified manufacturers. Kogan is retained as a named retail/marketplace business because it was a specific tool source.

### Teams, media, events and individuals

Motorsport personalities, media accounts, race teams, events and governing bodies were not reclassified as product competitors. Examples include MotiveDVD, Premiair Racing, Summernats and Speedcars Australia.

### Unadopted product names and unproven relationships

SkidLabz naming suggestions are not existing competitors. Possible OEM/private-label relationships, including a suspected factory behind a named brand, are not asserted without evidence.
